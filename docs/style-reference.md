# Style reference - Dragon Knight 4

Approved pilot: the prologue (batch b000, booted and checked in-game). Match these voices.

## Voices
| Character | Voice | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Lushifon | formal, proud demon noble; "Lord Minax" | "With respect, Lord Minax, would Mano and I not have come to this sooner or later?" |
| Minax | archaic, menacing demon lord; "Heh heh heh..." | "Hold your tongue..." / "Listen well, Lushifon..." |
| Powell | samurai-stiff; でござる = ", good sir" (decisions D7) | "I have picked up Seed of Power x2, good sir." |
| Mano | gentle, devoted | "Lushifon. Kiss me once more... And then... hold me..." |
| Kakeru (child) | cheeky brat | "Yaaay!! I saw 'em, I saw 'em!!" / "If your mommy comes, I'll put a frog down her back!!" |
| Kakeru (teen) | casual, upbeat boy; calls his father "Dad" | "Yeah, Dad... and I won again today." |
| Takeru | jovial, boisterous hero | "Wahaha! Of course there is... he's my son!!" |
| Luna | fussy, loving mother; baby talk to baby Kakeru | "Ye-es, Kakeru sweetie... Drinky lots and lots so you gwow up big and stwong!" |
| Natasha (child) | bratty little girl | "I'm gonna tell my mommy on you!!" |

## Pairs
- 遠い、遠い未来の時代…… -> "A far, far distant future..."
- 四天王のひとりであるお前が、私に隠れて娘と契りを結ぶとは……な。 -> "To think that you, one of the Four Heavenly
  Kings, would bind yourself to my daughter behind my back..."
- よし……叱るぞ、すぐに叱るぞ、いま叱るぞ。 -> "Right... I'll scold him, I'll scold him right away, I'm scolding him now."
  (keeps the comic triple)
- ちゅうちゅう……むしゃむしゃ。 -> "Suckle, suckle... chomp chomp." (sound words in English)
- Corrected example: ……。 alone was first "..." ; use "......" so a silent beat reads as a beat.
